# App shell

The app shell is the frame of every page of an application: the app bar at the top, the main navigation on the side, and the page in a `main` element, with a toolbar above its content.

## Layout

- Put the app bar at the top, over the full width.
- Put the main navigation on the left, with a width of 280px. See [Main navigation](?path=/docs/patterns-main-navigation--docs).
- Put the `main` element next to the main navigation: the toolbar at the top, and the content below it, with a padding of 24px, or 16px on small screens. See [Toolbar](?path=/docs/patterns-toolbar--docs).

Until `@dextinity/ui` has a component for the app bar, build it with your own elements, as in the full example.

## AI assistant

The AI assistant is the shell's third column, after the page. Build it with `AppShell`'s `assistant` and the [`AiAssistantSwitch`](?path=/docs/shell-top-bar-scope-aiassistantswitch--docs) in the [`TopBar`](?path=/docs/shell-top-bar-scope-topbar--docs)'s `assistantSwitch`.

- Put the switch after the scope selector and before the user menu.
- Give the assistant its own column. The page gets narrower; never cover the page with the assistant.
- Show and hide it with the switch and ⌘J / Ctrl+J. Move the focus into its composer when it opens, and back to the switch when it closes.
- Keep the conversation when the assistant is hidden and when the user navigates between pages.
- Name what the assistant works on in its header: the current page and the scope.
- On narrow windows, show only the switch's icon, and open the assistant as a bottom sheet.
- Leave out the switch and the assistant for users without the right to use the assistant.

See the [example with the assistant](?path=/story/shell-app-frame-appshell--with-assistant).
