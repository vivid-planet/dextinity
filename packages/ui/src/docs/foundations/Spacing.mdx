# Spacing, radii & material

Spacing scale, radii, shadows, glass, and density. Radii scale with the brand's radius factor `r`.

## Spacing

A grid in steps of 4, plus 18 for the gap between glass panels.

<SpacingScale />

## Radii

Every radius is multiplied by the brand's radius factor `r` (`--dextinity-brand-radius-factor`). Controls take their radius from the brand directly (`--dextinity-brand-radius-control`), so a brand can make them pills.

<RadiusScale />

## Shadows & material

Shadows and glass are token layers of their own. High contrast turns both off and draws lines instead.

<MaterialSamples />

## Density

A user setting. Fixed values per level; the font size stays the same and click targets never go below 24px. Highlighted: the density picked in the toolbar.

<DensityTable />

Durations and easing are on the [Motion](?path=/docs/foundations-motion--docs) page.
